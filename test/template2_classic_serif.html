About

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at Yale University and a Faculty Research Fellow at the NBER. I do research in Financial Economics and Macroeconomics, with an emphasis on normative questions. I teach General Equilibrium and Welfare Economics, Financial Economics, and Normative Macro-Finance.
