Eduardo Dávila, Assistant Professor of Economics at Yale University.

Welfare, Frictions, and the Design of Policy

From the Department of Economics

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at Yale University and a Faculty Research Fellow at the National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER). My research spans Financial Economics and Macroeconomics, with an emphasis on normative questions.

I teach General Equilibrium and Welfare Economics, Financial Economics, and Normative Macro-Finance. A full curriculum vitae is available here (PDF), along with profiles on Google Scholar and GitHub. Correspondence: eduardo.davila@yale.edu.
