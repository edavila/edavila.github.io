Website Template Previews

Fifteen self-contained homepage mockups with the site's real content. No external CSS or JavaScript — each file is a single standalone page. Templates 1–5 are conventional academic redesigns, 6–10 explore different visual directions, and 11–15 are themed concepts.

Round 1 — Academic redesigns

1 · Indigo Cards Modern

Light-grey page with white rounded cards, a profile sidebar with field pills, and journal badges. Modern report-style look.

Open

2 · Classic Serif Minimal

Centered single column in Palatino/Georgia with small-caps section headers and thin rules. Closest in spirit to a traditional academic page, but polished.

Open

3 · Fixed Sidebar Single-page

Dark Yale-blue sidebar with photo and navigation pinned on the left; content scrolls on the right. The layout used by many modern academic sites.

Open

4 · Hero Banner Bold

Full-width Yale-blue gradient header with photo, blurb, and quick-link pills, followed by paper cards with status badges and a sticky navigation strip.

Open

5 · Editorial Grid Magazine

Serif masthead with a heavy rule, then papers as a responsive two-to-three column grid of cards with uppercase journal kickers. Most visually distinctive.

Open

Round 2 — Different directions

6 · Terminal Dark

Dark macOS-style terminal window with shell prompts (whoami, cat drafts/recent.md), syntax-highlighting colors, and a blinking cursor.

Open

7 · Brutalist Loud

Thick black borders, hard offset shadows, huge uppercase type, and yellow highlight stamps. Raw and deliberately unpolished.

Open

8 · Broadsheet Newspaper

Old-style newspaper front page: small-caps masthead, double rules, three ruled columns, drop cap, bylines, and a sepia-toned photo with caption.

Open

9 · Swiss Poster Typographic

International-typographic-style poster: oversized uppercase Helvetica, numbered sections, ruled table rows, a red accent, and a grayscale photo on a strict grid.

Open

10 · Glass Gradient Soft

Pastel gradient background with frosted-glass panels (backdrop blur), a gradient-text name, rounded photo, and pill-shaped quick links.

Round 3 — Themed concepts

11 · Chalkboard Lecture

A wood-framed chalkboard with a chalk tray: hand-written chalk typography, a taped-up photo, dashed underlines, and sections framed as "Today's Lecture" and "Required Reading".

Open

12 · Working Paper LaTeX

The homepage typeset as an academic paper: title block, abstract with JEL codes, numbered sections, publications stated as Propositions, news as Remarks, and an asterisk footnote — on a white sheet over a grey desk.

Open

13 · Transit Map Subway

Papers as stations on three colored subway lines — Working Papers (orange), Publications (blue, "all stations peer-reviewed"), News (green) — with route bullets, transfer badges, and a service notice.

Open

14 · Trading Terminal Bloomberg

Black-and-amber financial terminal: a scrolling ticker of papers, a DAVILA <GO> command line, panels for profile and newswire, and publications as a "settled trades" table with ticker symbols.

Open

15 · Gallery Museum

A dark museum wall with a gilt-framed portrait and engraved plaques: working papers as "New Acquisitions", publications as "The Permanent Collection", each paper on its own museum label.
