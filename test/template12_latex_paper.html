Abstract

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at Yale University and a Faculty Research Fellow at the NBER. I do research in Financial Economics and Macroeconomics, with an emphasis on normative questions. I teach General Equilibrium and Welfare Economics, Financial Economics, and Normative Macro-Finance. This page collects recent drafts (Section 1), selected publications (Section 2), and news (Section 3). A full CV is available here.

JEL codes: D60, E61, G18. Keywords: welfare, financial economics, macroeconomics.
